Connect your Google account once

The player streams from your own Drive, so it needs permission to read it. Videos play in Google's player; transcripts are read through the Drive API.

  1. Open Google Cloud console and create a project, any name.
  2. In APIs & Services > Library, search Google Drive API and enable it.
  3. In APIs & Services > OAuth consent screen, choose External, fill in the app name and your email, and add your own Google address under Test users.
  4. In Credentials, create an OAuth client ID of type Web application. Under Authorized JavaScript origins add https://yakubuyinusat.github.io.
  5. Copy the client ID, it ends in .apps.googleusercontent.com, and paste it below.
